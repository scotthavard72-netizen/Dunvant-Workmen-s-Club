Frequently asked

Can non-members come in?

Yes — non-members are welcome as guests, though members get a discounted drink price.

Is the club child-friendly?

Children are welcome up to 9pm.

Is there parking?

There's a car park accessed via Voylart Road, roughly 100 metres from the club, below the front of the building near the mini-roundabout.

How do I become a member?

Ask at the bar — a committee member can talk you through it.

Can I hire the function room even if I'm not a member?

Function room bookings need a member as the nominated responsible adult present for the whole event.
