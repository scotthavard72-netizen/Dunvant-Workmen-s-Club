Alarm Fault
Who to call and what to do if the alarm won't set or trips
1

Stay calm, check the panel

Look at the alarm panel display for a zone number or fault code — this tells the engineer what's wrong and speeds things up.

2

If it won't set on close

Don't force it or leave the building unset. Try once more, then call the alarm company's fault line rather than guessing.

3

If it's gone off unexpectedly

Check nothing's actually wrong (break-in, fire) before assuming it's a fault. If all clear, silence and reset per the panel instructions, then still log it — repeated false alarms need investigating.

4

Log it

Let management know what happened, even if you sorted it yourself — patterns matter more than one-off faults.

Till Discrepancy
Steps for a count that doesn't match
1

Recount before anything else

Most discrepancies are counting mistakes. Recount the float and takings separately before assuming there's a real problem.

2

Check for obvious causes

Look back through the till roll for any voids, refunds, or manual overrides that might explain the gap.

3

Log the discrepancy

Write down the amount, the date, and who was on the till during that shift — even small discrepancies, so patterns can be spotted over time.

4

Notify management

Send the details via the Outlook group email. Don't try to "fix" the figures yourself — let management decide how to handle it.

When to escalate immediately

Any discrepancy over [amount] should be reported to management the same night, not left until the next shift.
