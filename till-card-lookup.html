Till & Card Lookup Guide

How to apply the member discount at the till

This is an example page — swap in the real steps for your actual till software once confirmed with C S Marketing.
1

Ask for their membership card

Every member should be able to show a card with a barcode on the back.

2

Scan the barcode

Use the USB scanner at the till — point and click, no need to press enter first.

3

Confirm the discount

The till screen should show the member's name and apply the discounted price automatically.

4

Ring it through as normal

Take payment as usual — the discount is already applied to the total.

If the card won't scan

Look the member up by name in the membership book behind the bar, or ask a keyholder for help. Never guess a discount without confirming membership.
