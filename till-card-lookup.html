Till & Card Lookup Guide

Finding a member on the till so their discount applies

How members are looked up on the club's till. Members get their discount once their account is on the sale — never guess a discount without finding them.
1

Ask for their membership card

Before ringing the drinks through.

2

Tap SWIPE CARD and swipe it

Their account is picked up and member prices apply to the sale.

3

No card, or it won't swipe? Search for them

Tap Manager, then Customer Number to open the member search. Type their surname or account number on the on-screen keyboard, check the Group column says they're a member, then tap their row. ESC or Back gets you out without picking anyone.

4

Ring it through as normal

Take payment as usual. Member discounts: 30p off a drink, 20p off a soft drink, 10p off a snack — see the Price List.

Member groups you'll see
  • Ordinary Members — standard membership
  • Senior Members — over-60s
  • Life Members — life membership
  • New Members — joined recently
  • Temporary Members — temporary membership
Selling a membership

Use the membership buttons on the till:

£20 New Members £10 Membership £8 Over 60's Membership £3.50 Pass Card Life Membership

Still can't find them?

Ask a keyholder or manager before giving a member price. Don't guess.
