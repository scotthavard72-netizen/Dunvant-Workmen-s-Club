Accident / Incident Report

For accidents, injuries and anything unsafe — not till issues

🚑 Someone badly hurt? Call 999 first and deal with them. Fill this in afterwards.
1. Make sure everyone's safe — first aid, call for help, keep others away.
2. Record it here as soon as you can, while it's fresh. Managers get an alert straight away.
3. If the club keeps a paper accident book, fill that in too.
4. Serious injuries may have to be reported by law (RIDDOR) — tell a manager in person or by phone, don't just leave it here.
