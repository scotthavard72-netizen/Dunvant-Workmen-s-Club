Till Discrepancy

Steps for a count that doesn't match

This is an example page — adjust the threshold and escalation step to match what management actually wants reported.
1

Recount before anything else

Most discrepancies are counting mistakes. Recount the float and takings separately before assuming there's a real problem.

2

Check for obvious causes

Look back through the till roll for any voids, refunds, or manual overrides that might explain the gap.

3

Log the discrepancy

Write down the amount, the date, and who was on the till during that shift — even small discrepancies, so patterns can be spotted over time.

4

Notify management

Send the details via the Outlook group email. Don't try to "fix" the figures yourself — let management decide how to handle it.

When to escalate immediately

Any discrepancy over [amount] should be reported to management the same night, not left until the next shift.
